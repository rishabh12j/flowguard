# warranty-worker crash loop after a settings change

## Symptoms

Work stopped while warranty-worker restarted repeatedly.

## Root cause

A release dropped a required setting.

## Resolution

The release was rolled back after approval.

## Follow-up

A restart alert was recommended. It was not implemented.
