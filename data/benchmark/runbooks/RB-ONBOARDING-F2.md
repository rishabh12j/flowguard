# Export rejected by onboarding-partner-sftp

## When to use

Use this runbook when onboarding-partner-sftp rejects a file.

## Procedure

1. Read the rejection reason.
2. Roll back the change that caused it, after approval.
3. Redeliver the file.

## Warnings

- Never email export files.
