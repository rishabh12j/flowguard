# Daily totals don't reconcile

## When to use

Use this runbook when rewards-reports' daily totals don't match.

## Procedure

1. Find the orders on the wrong day.
2. Check how their times were stored.
3. Fix the times and rerun the report.

## Warnings

- Never edit totals by hand.
